StrideProof

Proof-of-Workout Protocol — cryptographically verified sports activity for brand reward campaigns

The Problem

Up to 30% of activity in sports reward campaigns is fraudulent: GPS spoofing, emulators, borrowed devices. Brands pay for runs that never happened and cannot audit it afterwards.

The Solution

  • Ed25519-signed signals — every workout is cryptographically signed at the athlete's node. Change one byte and verification fails.
  • Proof-of-humanity — each node is bound to a unique human (nullifier hash). Sybil farms are excluded.
  • Quality scoring 0–100 — GPS coverage, anomaly detection, cadence and HR validity checked before signing.
  • Self-verification — the brand verifies signatures on its own side. Trust no one, verify everything.

How It Works

  • 1. Athlete's watch exports workout data (Polar / Garmin / Strava).
  • 2. StrideProof node scores data quality and builds a PSS-1 signal.
  • 3. Node signs the signal with its Ed25519 key.
  • 4. Brand receives the signal via webhook, verifies the signature, issues rewards by campaign rules.
